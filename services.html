About our photography services

At Golden Forest Studio, we specialize in capturing life’s most cherished moments with creativity and care. Whether it's a grand wedding, a romantic pre-wedding shoot, or a heartfelt anniversary, our team ensures every emotion is beautifully preserved. We also create joyful memories through kids portraits, cultural moments like upanayana and annaprashan, and vibrant birthday celebrations. Our expertise extends to covering events with precision and capturing the grace of motherhood in maternity shoots. For individuals and professionals, our tailored portfolio sessions deliver high-quality, expressive images. At Golden Forest Studio, every frame tells a story—your story.

About our videography services

Golden Forest Studio offers cinematic videography services that turn your special moments into timeless visual stories. From emotional wedding films and creative pre-wedding videos to lively anniversary, birthday, and kids events, we capture every detail with artistry and emotion. Our coverage of traditional ceremonies like upanayana and annaprashan is respectful, vibrant, and culturally rich. We also specialize in professional event coverage, heartfelt maternity films, and engaging portfolio reels for personal or professional branding. Enhance your story with our stunning drone shoots, offering breathtaking aerial perspectives that add a cinematic edge to your video. At Golden Forest Studio, we bring your moments to life—beautifully filmed, professionally edited, and truly unforgettable.
